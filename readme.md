Conclusions With the coexistence and overlap coverage of MBS and MIB, the collaborative perception of MBS and MIB has become a widespread research topic. In this paper, we consider the multi-target positioning problem of MBS and MIBS collaborative passive perception in the IoV scenario. To achieve high-precision positioning with limited resources, we propose symbol-level fusion methods and 3D-GDFT methods. Simulation results show that the MBS and MIBS collaborative passive perception scheme has good positioning performance.
